# Instructions for Preparing the Lab Assignment Report

(Data Warehouse and Data Mining – BCT IV/I 'C/D' )

Students are required to prepare a formal report documenting the work completed as part of the lab assignment.
The report must strictly follow the structure, organization, and formatting guidelines of the
research paper provided. Ensure that all relevant sections are included and presented in an
appropriate academic format.

### Please observe the following requirements:

• Read and Understand the research paper attached.
• Clearly document the work completed during the lab assignment.
• Follow the structure and format of the paper provided.
• Present the methodology, results, observations, analysis, and conclusions, wherever
applicable.
• Use your own words when describing your work and findings.
• Properly acknowledge and cite any external sources, references, figures, or materials
used.
• Carefully proofread the report before submission to ensure clarity, accuracy, and
academic quality.
• Encouraged to prepare report in Latex (Overleaf) and choose a format of the same
journal.

### Plagiarism and AI-Generated Content

All submitted reports will be checked for plagiarism and AI-generated content. If the
combined level of plagiarism or AI-generated content (including figures/captions) is found to
exceed 20%, the report will be rejected.
Such a rejection may negatively affect your overall performance and evaluation for the lab
assignment. Therefore, students are strongly advised to ensure that the report reflects their own
understanding, analysis, observations, and writing.
Please take this requirement seriously and submit an original, properly structured, and
academically sound report.
